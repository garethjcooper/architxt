# Entities Panel

The **Entities** panel is the left column of the Views page. It lists every grounded entity in the selected bank and lets you choose which entities and edges appear on the canvas.

## What is listed

- One row per grounded entity in the bank.
- Expanding an entity reveals its edges.
- The first level shows the grounded co-occurrence edges from Hindsight.
- The next level shows contextualised edges that were found for each grounded edge.

## Filters

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **All** | Show every entity in the bank. |
| **View** | Show only entities that are currently on the canvas. |
| **Search** | Filter entities and edges by name, id, or edge type. |

## Checkboxes

| Checkbox | Effect on the canvas |
|---|---|
| **Entity** | Include or exclude the entity. |
| **Grounded edge** | Toggle the whole grounded co-occurrence relationship at once. |
| **Contextualised edge** | Toggle an individual contextual edge underneath a grounded edge. |

When you select an edge, both of its endpoint entities are implied onto the canvas automatically.

## Find in panel

If you use the canvas find shortcut (`F3` / find) to locate an item on the graph, that item is also revealed in the Entities panel so you can inspect and toggle it from there.

## Edge hierarchy

Grounded co-occurrence edges appear first. The contextualised edges discovered for each grounded relationship appear underneath. This lets you turn on a whole relationship or inspect the individual edges that produced it.
