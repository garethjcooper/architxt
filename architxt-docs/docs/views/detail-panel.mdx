# Detail Panel

The **Detail** panel is the right column of the Views page. It loads the mental-model content behind whatever nodes or edges you have selected on the canvas.

## What is shown

| Selection | Content |
|---|---|
| **One or more nodes** | Attached plain, derived, node, and seed mental models for those entities. |
| **One or more edges** | Edge-context mental models that explain the relationship. |

## Envelope rendering

The content renders as a standard architxt envelope with narratives, tables, diagrams, and graphs. Slide the section into view to reveal the hover actions: index/plain-text toggle, evidence, and add-to-page.

## Actions

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| **Show evidence** | Opens the evidence modal for the section. |
| **Add to page** | Adds the section to an open Workspace curated page. |

## Toggling the panel

Use the panel toggle in the top bar or the column handle to open and close the Detail panel.
