# Directives

Directives in architxt are hard rules you write to shape what the system remembers and how it answers. A directive is a short **Name** plus a **Statement** — for example, *“Always capture data flows as separate facts”* or *“Never invent entity aliases that are not in the source text.”* Directives can be active or inactive, have a priority, and can be tagged.

They map to Hindsight's [`directives`](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/api/memory-banks#directives) concept and are enforced as [hard rules during Reflect](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/reflect#directives-hard-rules).

## Creating and managing directives

Open the **Directives** page from the main navigation. The page lists every directive with **ID**, **Name**, **Statement**, **Active**, **Priority**, **Tags**, **Generated By**, and creation time.

You can:

- Click **Add** to create a directive. A **Name** and **Statement** are required; **Active** defaults to on and **Priority** defaults to `0`.
- Click **Import** to paste a CSV of directives. The optional header is `directive_name,directive_statement`; optional columns include `is_active` and `priority`.
- Select one or more directives and click **Delete** to remove them.
- Search by name, statement, generated-by source, priority, or tag.

Click a directive row to open **Directive Details** and edit its name, statement, active state, priority, and tags.

### Active vs inactive

Only **Active** directives are sent to Hindsight. Inactive directives stay in architxt but do not participate in retention or reflection. Use this to draft or temporarily disable a rule without losing it.

### Priority

Priority is a numeric ordering hint. Higher numbers sort earlier. Use it when two directives might conflict or when one rule should be considered before another.

### Tags

Directives can be tagged like documents and mental models. Tags are useful for grouping rules by domain, project, or severity. Select one or more directives and click **Tags** to add or remove tags in bulk.

## Where directives are used

Directives are pushed to Hindsight memory banks as part of the bank's [`directives`](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/api/memory-banks#directives) configuration. Once there, they act as [hard rules for Reflect](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/reflect#directives-hard-rules): the model must follow them when generating answers, and they influence how facts are extracted and observations are formed.

You sync directives from the **Hindsight** page, alongside documents, entities, mental models, and bank settings.

## How directives fit into architxt

```text
     Directives
          │
          ├──────────────►  Hindsight Memory Bank
          │                       │
          │                       ▼
          └──────────────►  Reflect / Retain
```

Directives are global rules. They do not belong to a single document, but they travel with the bank configuration and apply every time the bank retains content or answers a Reflect query.

## Summary

Directives let you write explicit rules that govern extraction and reflection. Keep them active, ordered by priority, and tagged so they reach the right Hindsight banks and shape answers the way you intend.
